5. Nature of Mistakes

  • (a) Omission of words or insertion of foreign words → Major mistake.
  • (b) Omission of punctuation marks & incorrect punctuation marks → Minor mistake.
  • (c) Spelling mistake → Major mistake.
  • (d) Insertion of punctuation at the beginning / inside / end of a word → Major mistake.
  • (e) Small letter in place of capital or vice-versa (in the word) → Minor mistake.

6. Calculation of Marks of Accuracy

Marks = No. of Typed Words − (0.33 × Major Mistakes) − (0.15 × Minor Mistakes)

ℹ️ Special Rules Used by This Software

  • Key depressions: total characters typed (including spaces) ÷ 5 = number of words.
  • Un-typed / remaining part of the passage is NEVER counted as a major or minor mistake. (If you type 150 words out of 286, only your 150 typed words are evaluated.)
  • No space between two words: both those words are counted as major mistakes, and counting continues normally from the very next word — the following words / lines are not affected.
  • A word is counted only once; if a word has both a major and a minor error, it is counted as a major mistake.
  • Auto highlight is OFF and auto scroll is OFF — you scroll the passage yourself with the mouse / finger.
  • Copy-Paste, right-click and text selection are disabled. Changing tab is recorded.
